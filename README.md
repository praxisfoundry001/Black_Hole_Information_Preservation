# The Burden of Black-Hole Information Preservation

## An Event-Horizon Premise Challenge

This repository contains a scientific and formal challenge to one specific premise in the black-hole information problem:

**What establishes that the complete quantum evolution of an actual four-dimensional forming and evaporating black hole with a genuine event horizon preserves arbitrary initial quantum distinctions?**

The work does not argue that information is fundamentally destroyed.

It does not reject unitary quantum mechanics, holography, entanglement islands, quantum error correction, replica-wormhole calculations, gravitational reconstruction, Page-curve results, or mathematically controlled recovery theorems.

It asks whether the scope of those results has been shown to reach the complete physical process for which global information preservation is asserted.

## Main result

For the registered ordinary future-directed route from strictly inside a genuine event horizon to the exterior future, the stipulated horizon and Structural Flow translation premises imply that ordinary outward Cascade and same-route Authorization do not occur.

That route result does **not** determine the global fate of quantum information.

The restricted horizon-facing premises entail neither:

- guaranteed global quantum-information preservation; nor
- fundamental global information destruction.

Both physical fates therefore require additional physics-owned premises.

## Physical burden

A complete preservation claim must identify, at its declared scope:

- the actual physical target process;
- the admitted family of quantum inputs;
- the relevant physical evolution;
- the complete final physical observable system;
- preservation of the claimed quantum distinctions, including cross-input coherence where required;
- and the applicability of the mathematical result to the actual gravitational regime being claimed.

Recovery from Hawking radiation alone is not the only admissible proof route.

Likewise, failure to recover information from a restricted radiation subsystem is not sufficient to establish fundamental destruction.

## Falsification and reopening

The package contains a public falsification and reopening protocol that keeps several challenge seats distinct.

A matched failure of the event-horizon-to-Structural-Flow mapping reopens the **domain translation**.

A new model, reconstruction theorem, regulated operator, or code-level recovery result reopens the relevant **model or code burden**.

A physically established complete preservation or complete-output loss result changes the **physical information verdict** at its earned scope.

Only a genuine counterderivation reaching universal Structural Flow premises can reopen the implicated **universal Structural Flow claim** and potentially create Kernel or TOE pressure.

A black-hole-specific disagreement does not automatically propagate upward.
